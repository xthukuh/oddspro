# Sharp 4x: a daily 4x slip from live data (2026-10-15)

Owner ask: pull the live warehouse, analyse it without hindsight, and rebuild the safe-bets
strategy into something reliable for steady revenue, with a daily slip of at least 4x odds,
few legs, no Over 0.5 filler, and no daily maintenance.

Outcome: a **Sharp 4x** card ships on the daily slip (engine chapter 05). It is the first
selection in this project with a measured edge against a bookmaker's own probability. It is
not, and mathematically cannot be, a daily guarantee.

## Data and hindsight discipline

- Source: live host `oddsprok_prod_1_4_0`, read through a session with `tx_read_only = 1`
  (MariaDB refused writes, error `ER_CANT_EXECUTE_IN_READ_ONLY_TRANSACTION`).
- Extract: column-pruned, chunked by `odds_markets.id` ranges of 1M (0.16 s per probe chunk),
  gzip -9, md5-verified end to end. 91 MB core bundle plus 38 MB of BetPawa probabilities.
- Universe: 28,843 final (`FT/AET/PEN`) linked fixtures, 2026-07-02..10-15, 106 days.
- Dropped for hindsight: 280,051 price rows (2.2%) last written at or after the canonical
  kickoff (in-play prices), 20 bookmaker listings with reversed sides, all simulated
  competitions.
- Every replay is walk-forward: day D's picks use only fixtures settled on days before D;
  D's results are read only to settle D. First 21 days are warm-up, leaving 85 scored days.

Reproduce: `node scripts/sharp-prep.js` then `node scripts/sharp-backtest.js` (must print
85 published, 24 won).

## Findings, in the order they were established

1. **No stable raw-price edge.** Over 1.26M settled leg prices, one cell is positive in
   both halves (BetPawa away win at 1.35-1.60, +1.3% ROI). Everything else loses roughly
   the margin (`scripts/sharp-edges.js`).
2. **Line shopping backfires at closing.** When one book prices an outcome 5%+ above the
   other, that bet loses 13-19%: the outlier is usually the stale price, not the generous
   one.
3. **Arbitrage exists but is untradeable.** 730 cross-book arbs (sum of best inverse prices
   below 1) on 88 of 106 days, mean margin 3%. The 20% withholding on each winning bet's
   profit turns a 3% arb into about -7%.
4. **A single 4x card wins 20-25% of days under every model** without the bonus (85 days,
   `scripts/sharp-replay.js`); every calibration over-promised (predicted 25-27%).
5. **BetPawa's Win Bonus is formula-driven and public.** Its configuration sits in
   `GET /api/preference/v1/brand-component-data` (`bonusConfigurations.dynamicBonus`), its
   calculator in the site bundle (`com.betpawa.bonus.dynamic.v1..v5`). Transcribed to
   `src/db/betpawa-bonus-rules.js` and verified against the site's own v5 calculator run in
   its page: 1.05x / 1.08x / 1.45x / 2.649x and total odds 3.52 / 6.81 / 313.67 / 2139
   reproduced. With BetPawa's probability correct, punter EV stays below 1 by construction.
6. **BetPawa's own probability runs low.** Realized hit rate minus `q`, by price band, both
   halves positive: 1.02 +0.4, 1.10 +0.5, 1.20 +0.9, 1.35 +0.95, 1.60 +0.9, 2.20 +0.7,
   3.50 +0.55 points; 1X2 favourites at 1.35-1.60 +2.5 points. DNB shows a fake +18%
   because its `q` excludes the draw refund (excluded from everything).
7. **Walk-forward leg edge.** Legs ranked by the group|band residual beat `q` by
   r = 1.045 (SE 0.022, 2,545 legs; halves 1.029 / 1.053). The same holds at 1.5-2.5
   (1.045, halves 1.012 / 1.063). This is the load-bearing number.
8. **The shipped construction.** Exactly 3 legs at 1.30-2.00, slip >= 4x: 85/85 published,
   24 won (28.2%) vs BetPawa's 23.2%, 4.19x average with bonus, +18% gross (+29% / +7%),
   about 0% net of withholding. Looser price caps (2.5, 3.0) and 4-leg slips lost (-6% to
   -18%) in the same replay. The 2.00 cap is chosen for a structural reason (the edge sits
   in favourites at 1.35-1.60, and three legs near 1.59 make exactly 4x), and the choice
   among six tested constructions is a selection that the forward record must confirm.
9. **Most losses are draws.** In the last 14 days, 9 of 10 losing slips died on a draw.

## Why "guaranteed daily 4x" is proven unreachable here

A slip paying X wins with probability P, so its expected return is EV = P * X, which
gives P = EV / X. At X = 4 and the best measured EV (about 1.08-1.10 with the bonus),
P is at most about 27-28%. The replay sits on that bound (28.2%). Raising P toward 100% at
4x would need EV near 4, which no market here offers. What the data supports is a
positive-expectation 4x card that wins about one day in four, grown with disciplined
staking: a fixed small fraction of bankroll per slip (full Kelly at p 0.28, odds 4.2 is
about 5%; a quarter of that is the prudent stake).

## Taxes

Reported (industry press, not yet checked against the enacted text): 5% excise on deposits
(Finance Act 2025) and 20% withholding on betting winnings reinstated from 2026-07-01
(Finance Act 2026). BetPawa's brand config carries `whtDisplayType: WHT` and
`taxSystem.bonusView: COMPENSATION`. Every replay reports gross and net-of-withholding
side by side.
